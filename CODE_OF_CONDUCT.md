# Code of Conduct

## Contributor Covenant

We are committed to providing a welcoming environment for everyone. In our
projects, spaces, and communications we:

- Use inclusive language and assume good faith.
- Respect different cultures, viewpoints, and experience levels.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Accept responsibility and apologize to those we harm, and take steps to
  prevent recurrence.

Behavior that is harassment, hate speech, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, or publication of another person's private information is not
acceptable. Instances may be reported to the repository owner.

## Responsible-testing expectation

Contributors working on adversarial test cases must follow
`docs/methodology/responsible-testing.md`: synthetic targets only, tiered
approval gates, no real secrets or customer data, and controlled cleanup.
